# Homepage campaign imagery

The owner supplied two Kive.ai poster references on 2026-09-12 and rejected the earlier white product card. This version uses a single large racket, photographic lighting and full-width composition, with typography rendered as accessible web text.

## Provenance

- Generation mode: built-in image generation, with actual product photographs and the owner's second Kive reference as visual inputs.
- Higgsfield Soul 1080p / Realistic was attempted first and returned HTTP 403: Not enough credits. No job was created and no credit purchase was made. The built-in route was used after informing the owner.
- These are **AI editorial campaign visuals**, not official brand advertisements or faithful product documentation. The UI identifies the artwork and links separately to actual product photos and their sources.
- Real JPG imageUrl and source links remain unchanged. No sponsorship or endorsement is implied by showing a model. Existing sponsor disclosure only applies when a real sponsored record is provided.
- The Kive reference is used for composition and lighting only. Its text, watermark, feature claims and fictitious SPOTEN product are not reproduced.
- Delivery: 1536 × 1024 WebP, encoded at quality 88 using Sharp; original PNGs retained. No post-generation compositing or cropping.
